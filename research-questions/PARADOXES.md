# Paradoxes

*A paradox: two valid rules — or a rule and a standing request — that
contradict each other. The Chief Art Operator has the final say in everything
in the Art Operation @ The Historiotheque; the ruling is recorded here so the
contradiction never has to be re-litigated.*

## Paradox #1 — RQ numbering: sequential vs. never-backfill (2026-09-24)

**The conflict:** Rule A — "IDs move forward, never backfill" ("next RQ is 023",
recorded 2026-09-23) — vs. the Chief Art Operator's requirement that RQ numbers
follow the natural numbers sequentially. "I have 18 research questions, the
last two are 17 and 23" is absurd; the numbering must read 001–018.

**History:** RQ-2026-001–017 are the real questions. 018–021 were never issued
(an early miscount of 21, corrected 2026-09-23). 022 was issued, then retired
at the Chief Art Operator's request on 2026-09-23 (French dictation misfiled as
a question) — retired IDs are never reused.

**Ruling:** Rule A is overruled for this case. RQ-2026-023 → RQ-2026-018, before
anything was uploaded, so the rename is clean. 022 stays retired. Numbering
continues sequentially from the highest issued ID.
